# From Finance to Business Analytics

Hi, I'm **Mitisha Shah**, a Chartered Accountant
and MS Business Analytics and Information Management
student at Purdue University's Daniels School of Business.

With **8+ years of experience across finance, taxation,
auditing, and advisory**, I've developed a strong
understanding of financial decision-making and the
business challenges organizations face.

What has interested me most throughout my career is
finding ways to make complex financial processes
simpler, faster, and more effective through data.

This interest has shaped my transition into business
analytics, where I'm combining my finance background
with predictive modeling, programming, and
data-driven problem-solving.

My goal is to apply these skills in FinTech,
financial analytics, risk management, and

## My Professional Journey

### Combining Financial Expertise with Analytics

During my time at **G.P. Kapadia & Co**, I worked
across financial modeling, tax advisory, forecasting,
and process automation.

Some of the problems I worked on involved reducing
manual financial calculations, improving access to
business information, and using historical data to
support forecasting.

Highlights include:

- **80% reduction in manual effort** for capital gains
  computations through financial models.
- **75% reduction in person-hours** through SAP-based
  automated queries and dashboard refreshes.
- Developed an **ARIMA revenue forecasting model**
  achieving a normalized RMSE of **0.09**.
- Provided tax and regulatory advisory services to
  high-net-worth clients.
- Mentored interns and supported employee training
  and professional development.

Earlier, at **Shaparia Mehta & Associates LLP**, I
gained experience in financial auditing, regulatory
compliance, and working with different types of
organizations.

These experiences helped me develop a strong
foundation in analytical thinking, financial
problem-solving, and working with stakeholders.
